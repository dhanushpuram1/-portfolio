Project 1 Research · 2026

Context-Aware AI Agent

Predicts whether an online shopper is likely to purchase, then maps that probability to a practical next step such as a promotion, reminder, or no intervention.

PythonXGBoostFeature engineeringDecision rules
89.7%test accuracy reported for XGBoost
on the project dataset
Read more ＋

Problem: Customer tools often use behaviour alone and stop at a prediction.

Approach: Combine session behaviour and contextual features, compare Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost, then use probability thresholds to recommend an action.

Outcome: XGBoost reached 89.7% test accuracy and 0.924 AUC-ROC in the submitted paper. This is a research prototype, not a deployed commercial product.
